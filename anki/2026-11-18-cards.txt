"AI Mastery::K Inference engineering and hardware"	"[K04] How many parameters and FLOPs does a Grouped-Query Attention (GQA) projection block use compared to standard MHA?"	"Q uses 2*B*S*d_model^2 FLOPs, while K and V each shrink by n_kv_heads / n_heads (for example 2/4 = 0.5x in the kata, or 8/32 = 0.25x in Llama 3 8B)."	"K04 T1 W01"
"AI Mastery::K Inference engineering and hardware"	"[K04] What bug happens if you expand GQA K and V heads with tile instead of repeat_interleave across the head axis?"	"The tensor shape [B, S, H, d_head] still matches so no shape error fires, but query heads pair with the wrong KV head groups, silently corrupting logits (0.357 max delta in the kata)."	"K04 T1 W01"
"AI Mastery::K Inference engineering and hardware"	"[K04] Why does a SwiGLU MLP have three weight matrices instead of two, and what are their shapes?"	"It computes (SiLU(x W_gate) * (x W_up)) W_down, using two [d_model, d_ff] projections and one [d_ff, d_model] projection, costing 6 * B * S * d_model * d_ff FLOPs per layer."	"K04 T1 W01"
"AI Mastery::K Inference engineering and hardware"	"[K04] How do you save S-fold compute on the LM head during prefill when you only need the next token?"	"Slice the final normalized hidden state to x[:, -1:, :] of shape [B, 1, d_model] before multiplying by W_lm [d_model, V], cutting LM head FLOPs from 2*B*S*d_model*V to 2*B*d_model*V."	"K04 T1 W01"
"AI Mastery::K Inference engineering and hardware"	"[K04] Why must the causal mask add -inf (or -1e9) above the main diagonal BEFORE softmax rather than zeroing after softmax?"	"Adding -inf before softmax makes exp(-inf) = 0 while remaining rows still sum to 1.0; zeroing after softmax breaks row normalization and leaks future magnitude through the denominator."	"K04 T1 W01"
"AI Mastery::K Inference engineering and hardware"	"[K04] In Pre-RMSNorm residual blocks, what passes through the main highway x = x + sublayer(RMSNorm(x)) and why?" "AI Mastery::D Transformers and LLM internals"	"[D10] Why must temperature scaling (z / T) run before top-p or min-p truncation?" "AI Mastery::D Transformers and LLM internals"	"[D10] How does multiplicative repetition penalty handle positive versus negative logits for already-seen tokens?" "AI Mastery::D Transformers and LLM internals"	"[D10] Why does min-p outperform top-p when running at high temperature (T > 1) on a peaked distribution?"	"Top-p accumulates fixed mass p and pulls tail garbage in when p_max < p, whereas min-p cuts any token below min_p * p_max."	"D10 T1 W01"
"AI Mastery::D Transformers and LLM internals"	"[D10] What happens to the softmax distribution as temperature T approaches 0 versus infinity?" "AI Mastery::D Transformers and LLM internals"	"[D10] How do OpenAI-style frequency penalty and presence penalty differ in how they modify logits?"
