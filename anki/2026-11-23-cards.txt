"ITL_i = t_i - t_{i-1} is the individual gap between consecutive streamed tokens; TPOT is the request's arithmetic mean of all N_out - 1 ITL gaps."	"K08 T1 W02"

"AI Mastery::D Transformers and LLM internals"	"[D04] What is the exact formula and tensor shape trace for single-head causal self-attention on input X of shape (T, d_model)?"	"Q, K, V are (T, d_k); scores S = Q@K^T/sqrt(d_k) and weights A = softmax(S+M) are (T, T); output A@V is (T, d_v)."	"D04 T1 W02"
"AI Mastery::D Transformers and LLM internals"	"[D04] Why do we divide dot-product attention scores by sqrt(d_k) before applying softmax?"	"For unit-variance Q and K, dot-product variance is d_k; dividing by sqrt(d_k) restores unit variance so softmax does not saturate into one-hot weights with vanishing gradients."	"D04 T1 W02"
"AI Mastery::D Transformers and LLM internals"	"[D04] Why must masked future positions in causal self-attention be set to -infinity (or -1e9) before softmax rather than 0?"	"Because exp(0) = 1 gives future tokens positive weight after softmax normalization, leaking future context, whereas exp(-inf) = 0 guarantees zero attention weight."	"D04 T1 W02"
"AI Mastery::D Transformers and LLM internals"	"[D04] How do the FLOPs and intermediate attention matrix memory of self-attention scale with sequence length T and head width d_k?"	"Computing Q K^T and A V costs 4 T^2 d_k FLOPs, while materializing the T by T score and weight matrices costs O(T^2) memory."	"D04 T1 W02"
"AI Mastery::D Transformers and LLM internals"	"[D04] Why does an off-by-one or missing causal mask give near-100% validation accuracy during training but garbage during autoregressive generation?"	"During teacher-forced training, query i reads the ground-truth next token at column j = i + 1; during generation, future tokens do not exist yet."	"D04 T1 W02"
"AI Mastery::D Transformers and LLM internals"	"[D04] In the Softmax Jacobian, why does an unscaled dot product at d_k = 256 kill gradient flow to Q and K?"	"Unscaled scores have std sqrt(256) = 16, pushing max softmax probability p_max near 1.0 so the Jacobian factor p_max(1 - p_max) collapses toward zero."	"D04 T1 W02"
"AI Mastery::D Transformers and LLM internals"	"[D04] Why does token t = 0 ('Patient') always have 0.00 bits of causal attention entropy regardless of its query or key values?"	"Causal masking sets columns j > 0 in row 0 to -infinity, leaving only column 0 unmasked, so softmax assigns probability 1.000 to itself."	"D04 T1 W02"
"AI Mastery::D Transformers and LLM internals"	"[D04] How do Q, K, and V projections differ functionally from the static token embedding table W_E?" "AI Mastery::D Transformers and LLM internals"	"[D05] How do MHA, GQA, and MQA differ in their ratio of query heads n_q to key-value heads n_kv?"	"MHA uses n_kv=n_q (1:1); GQA partitions n_q query heads into n_kv groups so g=n_q/n_kv heads share each KV head; MQA uses n_kv=1 across all query heads."	"D05 T1 W02"
"AI Mastery::D Transformers and LLM internals"	"[D05] What is the exact formula for KV-cache memory in bytes, and how much does GQA-4 (n_kv = 8 vs 32) save at 32k context?"	"Bytes = 2*L*n_kv*d_h*S*B*b; cutting n_kv from 32 to 8 shrinks a 32-layer BF16 cache at 32k context 4x from 16.00 GiB to 4.00 GiB."	"D05 T1 W02"
"AI Mastery::D Transformers and LLM internals"	"[D05] Why must GQA broadcast KV heads using grouped repetition (repeat_kv) rather than interleaved tiling?"	"Pretrained checkpoints group adjacent query heads [g*k .. g*(k+1)-1] under KV head k; interleaving pairs query heads with the wrong KV subspace and scrambles outputs."	"D05 T1 W02"
"AI Mastery::D Transformers and LLM internals"	"[D05] How does Multi-head Latent Attention (MLA) reduce the KV cache even further than GQA?"	"MLA compresses each token into a shared latent c_KV (d_c=512) plus one decoupled RoPE key k^R (d_r=64), caching 576 floats/layer instead of 2*n_kv*d_h."	"D05 T1 W02"
"AI Mastery::D Transformers and LLM internals"	"[D05] How does MLA avoid materializing full multi-head keys and values in HBM during autoregressive decoding?"	"By matrix associativity, MLA absorbs W_UK into W_Q so queries project to d_c and dot directly with c_KV, and absorbs W_UV into W_O after pooling."	"D05 T1 W02"
"AI Mastery::D Transformers and LLM internals"	"[D05] Why does MLA require a separate decoupled RoPE key k^R instead of applying RoPE directly to the compressed latent c_KV?"	"RoPE multiplies by a position-dependent rotation matrix R(i - j) between Q and K, which breaks the constant linear associativity needed to absorb W_UK into W_Q."	"D05 T1 W02"
"AI Mastery::D Transformers and LLM internals"	"[D05] How do you uptrain an existing MHA checkpoint into a GQA checkpoint without training from scratch?"	"Mean-pool each group of g adjacent K and V projection head matrices into one KV head, then run a small fraction (~5%) of original pretraining tokens."	"D05 T1 W02"
