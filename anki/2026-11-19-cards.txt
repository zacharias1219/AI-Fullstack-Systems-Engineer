"AI Mastery::K Inference engineering and hardware"	"[K05] Why is LLM prefill compute-bound while single-stream decode is memory-bandwidth-bound?" "AI Mastery::K Inference engineering and hardware"	"[K05] What is the approximate FP16 linear-layer arithmetic intensity (FLOP/Byte) for prefill vs decode at batch size B and prompt length S?"	"Prefill is approximately B * S FLOP/Byte; decode is approximately B FLOP/Byte because 2*P weight bytes cancel in (2*T*P FLOPs) / (2*P bytes)."	"K05 T1 W01"
