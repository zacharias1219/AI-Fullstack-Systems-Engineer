The core idea

Most candidates read a job description like a flat wish list: they scan every bullet, worry about unfamiliar tools, and spend the week before the interview doing unfocused review. Because JDs mix HR templates, secondary stack glue, and real production fires in one list, unguided prep treats a zero-weight buzzword and a Tier 1 loop gate as equal priorities.

Treat every JD as a noisy telemetry log emitted by a team with a specific production bottleneck. Strip the 70% HR boilerplate, classify the role into one of four interview loop archetypes, map technical signals to exact Topic IDs, and spend 100% of your 7-day sprint on the top three gaps ranked by Return on Investment (ROI = weight * (1 - mastery)).

Memory hook

S-M-R-S-P (Strip, Map, Rank ROI, Sprint, Probe): Strip 70% HR noise, Map to 4 archetypes and 6 to 8 Topic IDs, Rank gaps by w * (1 - m), Sprint 14h on the Top 3 gaps, and Probe the interviewer with bottleneck-hypothesis questions.

How it works

1. The 3-bucket JD decoder and the 4 loop archetypes

Split every JD bullet into three buckets: Bucket 1 is Load-Bearing Bottlenecks (15% to 20% of bullets, Tier 1 weight w = 3.0) such as TTFT/TPOT, PagedAttention, hybrid RAG, or HIPAA VPC isolation. Bucket 2 is Stack Table Stakes (10% to 15%, Tier 2 weight w = 2.0) like FastAPI SSE or voice pipelines. Bucket 3 is HR Boilerplate (65% to 75%, weight 0.0) like "passionate team player in a fast-paced environment." Drop Bucket 3 immediately. The 6 to 8 surviving Topic IDs classify the role into one of four loop archetypes:

  • Inference Systems (K-heavy: K04, K05, K06, K07, K14, K20): GPU roofline math, KV cache paging, FP8/AWQ quantization, and TTFT/TPOT batching tradeoffs.
  • Applied LLM, RAG, and Agents (G, H, I, J: G01, G04, H01, I02, J01): Prefix caching, hybrid BM25 + dense retrieval, agent step budgets and tool guards, and golden-set evals.
  • AI Fullstack and Platform (D, M, N, S): Async SSE backpressure, multi-tenant gateways, semantic caching, and token cost attribution.
  • Forward Deployed Engineer / FDE (G, H, J, S, T, V, W, X06): Messy customer workflow decomposition, VPC/PII erasure, unit economics per 1k requests, and stakeholder discovery.
Check: A job description lists "FastAPI, Kubernetes, RAG chunking, LLM-as-judge evals, tool-calling state machines, and prompt prefix caching." Which archetype loop is this, and which Topic IDs dominate the technical deep dive?
It is Archetype 2 (Applied LLM, RAG, and Agent Systems) with a light Fullstack (M02) wrapper. The load-bearing deep-dive IDs are H01 (RAG chunking), J01 (LLM-as-judge evals), G04/I02 (tool calling and agent loops), and G01 (prefix caching).

2. Readiness math and Gap-ROI sprint allocation

For extracted Topic IDs i = 1..k with loop weight w_i and current tracker mastery m_i (from 0.0 to 1.0), compute weighted readiness and each topic's Gap ROI:

Readiness % = 100 * sum(w_i * m_i) / sum(w_i)
Gap ROI_i   = w_i * (1 - m_i)
Sprint hours h_i (Top 3 gaps) = H_total * ROI_i / sum_top3(ROI)

Loops fail you on your lowest Tier 1 floor, not your average across ten bullets. A Tier 1 gap at 20% mastery (w = 3.0, m = 0.20, ROI = 2.40) gives 5.3x more leverage per hour than reviewing a learned Tier 1 topic at 85% mastery (ROI = 0.45), so spend 100% of a 14.0-hour sprint on the Top 3 ROI gaps.

3. Screen phrases, reverse-interview role-play script, and rubric

Use A/B probes on early calls to verify your loop map, and tie answers to your own systems (clinic voice AI, GDPR/HIPAA erasure, SSM edge constraints):

  • Recruiter screen: "To prepare the right system examples, are the technical rounds weighted more toward GPU serving and memory math, or toward application-layer RAG, eval harnesses, and agent orchestration?"
  • Hiring-manager screen: "Over the first 90 days, is the main engineering fire cutting p99 TTFT and token spend at scale, or raising retrieval recall and golden-set eval coverage on messy domain data?"
  • Proof bridge: "That mirrors the latency-versus-compliance boundary I hit on realtime clinic voice pipelines and HIPAA erasure workflows, keeping turn latency under 600 ms while stripping PHI before any vector write."

"""X15 Kata: Deterministic JD-to-Topic-ID Mapper, Readiness & Gap ROI Scorer."""

# Break-it knobs: flip one at a time, predict the effect, then run
INCLUDE_BOILERPLATE = False  # Break 1: True scores HR buzzwords with same weight as T1 core
EQUAL_WEIGHT_STUDY = False   # Break 2: True divides 7-day prep evenly instead of by gap ROI

# Topic catalog: tid -> (name, tier_weight, current_mastery 0.0..1.0)
# Candidate state: Week 1 foundational topics learned (0.85-0.90), deeper weeks partial (0.15-0.45)
TOPICS = {
    "K04": ("One token end to end (prefill/decode)", 3.0, 0.85),
    "K05": ("GPU memory & roofline arithmetic intensity", 3.0, 0.40),
    "K06": ("KV cache math, GQA & MQA sizing", 3.0, 0.85),
    "K07": ("PagedAttention & continuous batching (vLLM)", 3.0, 0.25),
    "K14": ("Quantization: INT8, FP8, AWQ, GPTQ", 3.0, 0.20),
    "K20": ("Serving SLOs: TTFT, TPOT & goodput", 3.0, 0.30),
    "G01": ("Token optimization & prompt prefix caching", 3.0, 0.90),
    "G04": ("Structured outputs & tool calling schemas", 3.0, 0.45),
    "H01": ("RAG architecture, chunking & hybrid BM25+dense", 3.0, 0.35),
    "I02": ("Agent loop budgets, timeouts & tool guards", 3.0, 0.25),
    "J01": ("Offline golden sets & LLM-as-judge evals", 3.0, 0.20),
    "M02": ("FastAPI async SSE streaming & backpressure", 2.0, 0.50),
    "S04": ("PII redaction, HIPAA/GDPR erasure & VPC isolation", 3.0, 0.65),
    "T01": ("Voice & realtime audio streaming pipelines", 2.0, 0.70),
    "V02": ("Unit economics: cost per 1k requests & gross margin", 2.0, 0.75),
    "W01": ("Tactical empathy, stakeholder alignment & discovery", 2.0, 0.80),
    "X06": ("FDE decomposition: messy workflow to 2-week PoC", 3.0, 0.30),
    "HR0": ("Fast-paced passionate team player synergy", 2.0, 0.10),
}

# Keyword signal -> Topic ID mapping
SIGNALS = [
    ("kv cache", "K06"), ("pagedattention", "K07"), ("vllm", "K07"),
    ("quantization", "K14"), ("fp8", "K14"), ("ttft", "K20"), ("tpot", "K20"),
    ("roofline", "K05"), ("hbm", "K05"), ("prefill", "K04"),
    ("prefix caching", "G01"), ("token cost", "G01"), ("tool calling", "G04"),
    ("hybrid search", "H01"), ("rag", "H01"), ("agent loop", "I02"), ("guardrail", "I02"),
    ("eval", "J01"), ("golden set", "J01"), ("sse", "M02"), ("fastapi", "M02"),
    ("hipaa", "S04"), ("pii erasure", "S04"), ("vpc", "S04"), ("voice", "T01"),
    ("unit economics", "V02"), ("stakeholder", "W01"), ("ambiguous workflow", "X06"),
    ("passionate", "HR0"), ("fast-paced", "HR0"),
]

JDS = [
    ("Role 1: Inference & Serving Systems Engineer", "Inference Systems",
     "Own vLLM serving and PagedAttention kernels; optimize KV cache sizing, FP8 quantization, "
     "HBM roofline utilization, prefill vs decode split, and strict TTFT and TPOT SLOs in a fast-paced team."),
    ("Role 2: AI Product / Fullstack & Agent Engineer", "Applied LLM & Agent Fullstack",
     "Build FastAPI SSE streaming backends, hybrid search RAG, tool calling schemas, agent loop "
     "guardrails, prefix caching for token cost, and golden set evals with passionate builders."),
    ("Role 3: Forward Deployed AI Engineer (Healthcare)", "Forward Deployed (FDE)",
     "Decompose ambiguous workflow problems with hospital stakeholders; deploy clinical RAG, voice "
     "pipelines, HIPAA VPC isolation, PII erasure, eval harnesses, and unit economics in a fast-paced org."),
]

PREP_HOURS = 14.0  # 7-day pre-loop sprint at 2.0 focused hours/day

print(f"=== X15 JD-TO-TOPIC MAPPER & GAP ROI SCORER (7-Day Budget: {PREP_HOURS:.1f}h) ===")
print(f"Flags: INCLUDE_BOILERPLATE={INCLUDE_BOILERPLATE} | EQUAL_WEIGHT_STUDY={EQUAL_WEIGHT_STUDY}\n")

for title, archetype, text in JDS:
    low = text.lower()
    matched = []
    for kw, tid in SIGNALS:
        if tid == "HR0" and not INCLUDE_BOILERPLATE:
            continue
        if kw in low and tid not in matched:
            matched.append(tid)
    tot_w = sum(TOPICS[t][1] for t in matched)
    ready_w = sum(TOPICS[t][1] * TOPICS[t][2] for t in matched)
    readiness = (ready_w / tot_w) * 100.0
    # ROI = weight * (1 - mastery)
    gaps = sorted([(TOPICS[t][1] * (1.0 - TOPICS[t][2]), t) for t in matched], reverse=True)
    top3 = gaps[:3]
    top3_roi_sum = sum(r for r, _ in top3)
    print(f"{title} [{archetype}]")
    print(f"  Extracted IDs ({len(matched)}): {', '.join(matched)}")
    print(f"  Weighted Readiness: {readiness:5.1f}% | Total Gap ROI: {sum(r for r, _ in gaps):.2f}")
    for rank, (roi, tid) in enumerate(top3, 1):
        name, w, m = TOPICS[tid]
        hrs = (PREP_HOURS / len(matched)) if EQUAL_WEIGHT_STUDY else (PREP_HOURS * roi / top3_roi_sum)
        print(f"  Gap #{rank}: {tid} ({name[:32]:32s}) | M={int(m*100):2d}% | ROI={roi:.2f} | 7d alloc={hrs:4.1f}h")
    # Simulated loop pass probability: baseline + gain from hours on top gaps
    gain = sum((PREP_HOURS / len(matched) if EQUAL_WEIGHT_STUDY else PREP_HOURS * r / top3_roi_sum) * r * 1.45 for r, _ in top3)
    pass_prob = min(95.0, readiness * 0.75 + gain)
    print(f"  Projected 7-Day Loop Readiness Score: {pass_prob:5.1f}%\n")

V01: What four elements must an engineer-voiced answer contain before you add any one-sentence business outcome?
A concrete mechanism (how it works), a specific number (latency, memory, ratio, or cap), a failure mode (where it breaks), and a tradeoff (what you pay for the gain).
X01: What are the three levels and exact time budgets of a 90-second technical interview answer?
Level 1 Outcome (20 seconds: what it does and why it matters), Level 2 Mechanism (45 seconds: step-by-step how it works), and Level 3 Internals and limits (25 seconds: numbers, failure modes, and boundaries).

Weak model answers lower your 7-day cold pass rate p, which increases the retry term 1/p (for example dropping p from 0.80 to 0.40 jumps the multiplier from 2.25 to 3.50, a 56% workload increase).
